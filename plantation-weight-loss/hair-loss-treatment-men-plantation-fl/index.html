    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Plantation, FL

    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Plantation

    Male pattern hair loss runs on DHT. DHT is a hormone made from testosterone, and in men who inherited a sensitivity to it, DHT slowly shrinks follicles at the temples and crown until they make thin, short hairs or none at all. Cutting DHT with finasteride protects follicles from further shrinkage. Minoxidil works on a separate track, lengthening the growth phase.

    Who Men's Treatment Is For

    It suits adult men with a receding hairline, thinning crown or diffuse thinning on top. It works best when there is still visible hair in the thinning areas, because follicles that have been bald for years rarely respond to any medicine. Earlier is better. Patchy bald spots, scalp pain or rapid loss call for a dermatologist visit. Your photos confirm the pattern.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Plantation

    Finasteride is one 1 mg tablet a day, with or without food. Topical minoxidil is applied to a dry scalp once or twice daily, while low-dose oral minoxidil is an off-label daily pill some providers prescribe instead, typically starting around 1.25 to 2.5 mg. Compounded sprays that combine finasteride and minoxidil go on once daily. Dutasteride is a capsule taken daily.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Months one and two can bring extra shedding, especially with minoxidil. The early shed is usually a sign follicles are cycling, and it fades. By month six, loss typically slows and density starts to improve. Fuller results show between nine and twelve months.

    Side Effects of Men's Treatment in Plantation

    Finasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in roughly one to two percent of men in trials. These usually resolve after stopping, and many men who notice them find they fade even while staying on the medicine. Rarely, men report low mood, which should be reported right away. Topical minoxidil can dry or irritate the scalp. Oral minoxidil can cause extra body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure and every medication you take. They also ask directly about past depression, current sexual health and any plans to have children soon, since those shape which medicine is chosen. PSA readings drop on finasteride. Tell any doctor who checks your prostate that you take it.

    How Men's Options Compare in Plantation

    Finasteride plus minoxidil is the standard starting pair for men. Dutasteride with minoxidil is a stronger DHT-blocking option, used off-label, for men who did not get enough from finasteride after about a year. Men avoiding DHT blockers can use a compounded minoxidil and GHK-Cu topical. Transplants help some men.
